Locked mission directives:

A navigation directive means a world-frame place. Heading is atan2(goal_y - pelvis_y, goal_x - pelvis_x). Set gait.mode=walk, gait.heading to that angle, and gait.speed to something the robot can hold. yaw is a standing torso offset and is ignored while walking.
On every turn, check that the robot is still on the way to the directive before you retune. The full-state snapshot has linear vel xyz (world frame) and planar speed (unsigned). Those do not say the walk is succeeding. Read along heading and closing speed. Along heading positive means the pelvis is moving the way gait.heading points; negative means it is walking backwards. Closing speed positive means it is moving toward the active goal; negative means it is moving away. A pelvis that is closer than where it started is not progress if closing speed is negative now. If either speed is negative, do not lower friction or slow the gait as though the approach were working. Re-aim gait.heading at the goal, or stop and stand, then start the walk again.

The call is not instantaneous. The robot keeps moving while you are invoked, and a periodic check also runs every 30 s if no listener fires. Arm the condition early enough that you still have time to act.

- gait.heading is a goal. The feet turn at gait.turn_rate, and a large heading error cuts speed to a crawl. Setting gait.mode=walk with speed and heading omitted defaults to about 0.25 m/s.

Gait for terrain and friction (use these before retuning locked QP costs):
- Low friction / slip: lower mpc.friction_mu and wbc.friction_mu toward the surface you believe. Slow down (gait.speed ~0.10–0.20), shorten gait.step_length_max, raise gait.duty_factor (more double support), widen gait.stance_width slightly, and lower gait.walk_accel. Error tracking policy:
- Error_Tracking is how you measure mission failure (weighted excess outside per-term deadbands).
- Modes: below_target, above_target, abs_above, abs_deviation. Value is offset + signal[index], or a named frame signal (heading_error, cross_track). Error is 0 inside the deadband.
- pelvis_height reads qpos[2] (pelvis world Z, ~0.78 when tall). It is not Task_Targets.height. When crouching, lower the below_target floor; do not set it equal to the CoM height command.
- While walking, world-x drift is not a failure. heading_error is held at 0 until the turn's rate budget elapses, so a turn in progress is not itself a breach. cross_track is lateral drift in the travel frame.
- Include Error_Tracking whenever the directive or the operating point changes. On a pure fault episode, include it only if the success criteria themselves should shift.
- There is no pitch-angle term and no motor-torque-versus-expected term unless you add one from qpos/qvel/frame. Torque shows up in section 2 (motor load), not as a default mission term.
- Keep thresholds loose enough for normal gait noise and tight enough to catch a real miss.

- object_type: "actuator", "geom", or "body"
- name: exact MJCF identifier (casing is tolerated)
- attribute: "gear" (actuator), "friction" (geom), "mass" (body), "com" (body)

- Model mutations describe reality; they do not override it. Never increase actuator gear to "fix" a dead or seized motor.
- Use section 0 (Tracked State) for mission criteria, section 2 (Motor Load) for effort, section 4 (Full State) for where the robot actually is.
- Do NOT blanket-adjust every actuator. Target the body or joint the telemetry implicates.

Belief-parameter search (unbounded binary search):
When a scalar belief (mass, friction, gear, one COM axis) is the hypothesis and the true value is unknown, do not creep. Bracket, then bisect.
- Round 1: jump. Mass that looks too light: try ~2× the live believed mass. Friction that looks too high: drop μ by ~3–10×. Weak motor: cut gear substantially, or zero it if it looks dead. COM: shift several centimeters along the implicated axis, not 1–2 cm.
- Later rounds: read ATTEMPTED FIXES. Undershoot = same error direction as before. Overshoot = the error flipped or a new opposite symptom.
- Undershoot and you have no bound yet: double (mass ← 2× last mass; |COM offset| ← 2× last |offset|; friction ← last/2).
- Overshoot: the last two values are a bracket. Next value is the midpoint.
- Stay on this search until the tracked-state error shrinks and stays down, or telemetry falsifies the kind of fault. A failed small step is not a falsified kind.
- Record the search in Analysis: last value, undershoot vs overshoot, next value and why.
