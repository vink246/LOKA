\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What LOKA is---and is not.}
LOKA is an online immune / adaptation layer over a certified locomotion plant.
It is not a VLA, not unsupervised skill discovery, and not a claim that language models outperform hierarchical MPC on recoverable push impulse.
Its comparative advantage is mission continuity under discrete change and operator language, with auditable typed mutations.

\paragraph{Interpretability.}
Hypotheses and analyses in the scratchpad provide a human-readable audit trail of recovery strategies, complementing numeric weight and model edits.

\paragraph{Latency and rate.}
LLM calls are orders of magnitude slower than MPC.
LOKA is therefore a supervisory layer for sparse events; asynchronous dual-rate execution---and classical reflexes for the catch window---are essential for stability during inference.

\paragraph{Safety and validity.}
Structured parsing, MJCF name resolution, hard allowlists, and affordance clamping reduce free-form action risk, but incorrect mutations can still destabilize the plant.
Harder verification, sandboxing, and human approval remain important for hardware deployment.
Belief gear alone does not retarget SRBD force plans; amputation recovery requires WBC authority edits and/or reflexes.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
Results depend on prompt quality and the chosen LLM; hallucinations of invalid names can still occur.
The mutation vocabulary is intentionally small.
Phase~A standing often shows limited \emph{fall-rate} gains because the plant already rejects many continuous disturbances; walking, limp-after-reflex, and adaptation memory are the regimes where we expect clearer LOKA$>$plant-only curves.
