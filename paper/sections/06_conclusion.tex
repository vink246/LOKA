\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

LOKA couples an LLM semantic orchestrator with a certified humanoid locomotion plant to adapt costs, gait policy, task goals, mission criteria, and internal-model belief under faults and operator intent---without placing language models in the millisecond catch path.
By compressing telemetry, isolating inference on a slow thread, gating unproductive retuning, and requiring multi-turn hypothesis revision through a typed YAML interface, LOKA aims to make adaptive locomotion both interpretable and operator-addressable, and theoretically more robust than code-as-policy solutions for underactuated bipeds.
Future work includes classical reflex catalogs with immune memory promotion, multi-embodiment adapters, stronger verification of proposed mutations, closed-loop hardware trials, and richer dynamics-parameter identification within the same orchestration loop.
